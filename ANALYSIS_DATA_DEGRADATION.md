# Data Degradation Analysis

We have implemented a suite of metrics to track the degradation of synthetic corpora over recursive generations, to establish the causal driver for model collapse and delayed grokking.

## Metrics
1. **Entropy:** Shannon entropy of the vocabulary over the targets. Lower entropy means loss of diversity.
2. **Distinct-n:** Ratio of unique n-grams to total n-grams. Lower value indicates increasing repetition.
3. **Compression Ratio:** Size of gzipped data divided by original text length. Redundant data compresses better (lower ratio).
4. **Duplicate Fraction:** Fraction of token repetitions in the sequence.
5. **Jensen-Shannon Divergence:** Distributional distance between current generation's target distribution and the original uniform target distribution.

## Results
We simulated up to 10 generations of collapse. The `pure` condition maintains high entropy and distinctness. As `collapse_severity` increases:
- **Entropy** decreases sharply.
- **JS Divergence** increases, showing a clear drift from the original uniform distribution.
- **Compression Ratio** plummets, indicating high redundancy.


## Correlation with Grokking
The degradation in **Entropy** and increase in **JS Divergence from Base** correlate strongly with delayed or failed grokking. As the target distribution becomes narrower (lower entropy) due to recursive collapse, the model is unable to escape the memorization phase, because the sparse representation required for the true algorithm is no longer adequately supervised by the degraded labels.
